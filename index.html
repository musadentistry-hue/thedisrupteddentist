The shift

Patients won't stop coming.
Why they come will change.

The book is part diagnosis of a breaking model, part field guide for the dentists who will build what comes next.

Yesterday · Old dentistry
Disease → Repair → Replace

A profession organized around damage: wait for it, fix it, then replace what can't be fixed. Fillings, crowns, root canals, extractions, dentures, implants.

Tomorrow · New dentistry
Predict → Prevent → Preserve → Optimize
  • PredictAI, advanced diagnostics and genetics that see risk before disease.
  • PreventBehavior, nutrition and biology working before the drill does.
  • PreserveMinimal intervention and regenerative dentistry that keep what's natural.
  • OptimizeFunction, aesthetics and longevity of the natural dentition for life.
Not broken.
Not replaced.
Awakened.

The author

An outsider who watched the model rise, and watched it break.

Mauricio Schneider has spent over three decades in clinical practice and dental education. Trained in Brazil, he built his career in the United States from outside the traditional pathway, earning his place one exam, one patient and one classroom at a time.

"The way it's always been done is not a rule. It's a habit."

Today he teaches prosthodontics at Nova Southeastern University College of Dental Medicine and runs a private implant practice in Weston, Florida, where he works at the intersection of digital dentistry, artificial intelligence and patient experience.

The Disrupted Dentist is his manifesto for a profession on the edge of reinvention.
